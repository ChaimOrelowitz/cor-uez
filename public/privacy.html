Privacy Policy

COR Solutions · Effective November 11, 2026

COR Solutions ("we", "us") helps businesses apply for New Jersey Urban Enterprise Zone (UEZ) certification and related grants through uez.corsolutions.io (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us when you sign up or apply: business details, owner contact information, identification and tax details, and documents you upload.
  • Messages you exchange with us by email, text or phone.
  • Basic technical information such as page visits, used to keep the Service working.

How we use it

  • To prepare, submit and track your applications with the State of New Jersey, the Lakewood Development Corporation and other agencies involved in your application.
  • To communicate with you about your application and our services.
  • To keep records of the work performed for you.

How we share it

We share your information only with the government agencies and program administrators your application requires, and with service providers that host and operate the Service (such as hosting, database and email providers) under obligations to protect it. We do not sell your information.

Google user data

The Service connects to Google accounts owned by COR Solutions so we can send email to our clients and keep a record of our conversations with them in one place. Through Google APIs, the Service sends messages and reads messages exchanged with our clients, including attachments and Google Voice notifications. This data is used only to provide and keep a record of our client communications. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the Service or as required by law.

The Service's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations.
